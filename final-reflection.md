# Final Reflection

Looking back on this course, I can clearly see how much I have grown both professionally and spiritually. Throughout each assignment, I developed new technical skills while strengthening the values that guide my decisions and actions.

When I began this course, my primary goal was to improve my technical abilities and prepare for a successful career in Information Technology. While those goals remain important, my understanding of what it means to become a trusted technology professional has grown significantly.

Working on projects involving AWS, Windows Server, Linux, Python programming, cybersecurity, cloud computing, and accessibility demonstrated that technology is ultimately about serving people. Every technical solution should be created with integrity, responsibility, and consideration for those who will use it.

My professional experiences also reinforced these principles. Supporting accessibility initiatives taught me that technology can remove barriers and create opportunities for everyone. This strengthened my desire to build solutions that are ethical, inclusive, and meaningful.

Spiritually, this course reminded me that faith and professional growth are not separate parts of my life. The principles of honesty, service, perseverance, and continual improvement apply equally in the workplace and in my personal discipleship. I have learned that becoming a capable Information Technology professional also means becoming someone others can trust.

Looking forward, I plan to continue expanding my technical knowledge while remaining committed to ethical leadership and lifelong learning. I hope to use my education and experience to help organizations solve complex problems, improve accessibility, and make technology a positive influence in people's lives.

This portfolio represents more than a collection of assignments. It reflects my journey, my growth, and my commitment to continually becoming a better student, professional, and disciple of Jesus Christ.
