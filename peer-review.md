# Peer Review

Peer reviewing another student's portfolio gave me the opportunity to evaluate their work, provide constructive feedback, and reflect on ways to improve my own portfolio. This experience helped me better understand the importance of organization, clear communication, and presenting technical work professionally.

---

# Portfolio Reviewed

For this assignment, I reviewed the portfolio created by **Jonah Green**.

**Portfolio Website:**  
🔗 https://jgreenportfolio.my.canva.site/

---

# Positive Feedback

Jonah created a well-organized portfolio that is easy to navigate and visually appealing. The different sections clearly separate his spiritual growth, technical projects, and professional experiences, making the website simple to follow.

One of the strongest parts of his portfolio is the **Project Gallery**, which includes documents from his coursework. This allows visitors to view evidence of his work rather than only reading project descriptions. His reflections also demonstrate meaningful connections between his spiritual principles and his professional development.

### Strengths

- Clean and organized layout
- Easy navigation between sections
- Meaningful spiritual reflections
- Strong collection of technical projects
- Effective use of supporting documents

---

# Suggestions for Improvement

Although the portfolio is already well designed, I believe one improvement could make it even stronger.

- Verify that all embedded documents have the correct sharing permissions. One of the embedded Canva documents in the Peer Review section appears to be unavailable.

---

# What I Learned

Reviewing another student's portfolio gave me several ideas for improving my own website. I especially liked the way Jonah organized his projects and included downloadable documents to showcase his work.

After completing this peer review, I updated my own portfolio by adding additional project documentation, expanding my project descriptions, and organizing my technical projects more effectively. These improvements make it easier for visitors to understand the skills I developed and the work I have completed throughout my Information Technology program.

This experience reinforced the importance of learning from others, accepting constructive feedback, and continually improving both technical and communication skills.
